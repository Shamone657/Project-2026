How do forests affect ecosystems and people?

Forests cover around a third of the land on earth, which absorbs 2.4 billion metric tons of carbon each year, housing 80% of amphibians, 75% of birds and 68% of mammals all over the world and provides 1.6 billion people who depend on forests food and fuel. Wood is a valuable resource that makes items such as paper and furniture.[0]

What is modelling and simulating?

Modelling is the representation of any design or idea, such as a real-world system, which can be used to analyse the effects of changes on the models if certain properties are altered.
Simulation is the process of running the model to gather information of the system itself and its performance.

Existing devices

There are wildfire detection devices that ranges from detecting wildfires before they begin to ones that can only detect once the wildfire has started. Early warning systems (EWS) are devices that take in data, process and analyse it and identifies if a risk is close to happening (‘close’ is a term that can change drastically between each device/company’).[1]

Some satellites made by the Swedish government have a visible infrared imaging radiometer suite (VIIRS) instrument which can detect fires once they have started using infrared rays and is able to contact authorities while the wildfire is small.[2]

The Silvanet Wildfire sensor is a more compact device that can be placed on trees which collects data such as the presence of gasses such as oxygen and carbon dioxide, temperature, humidity and air pressure.[3]

My decision

After researching the two EWS for wildfire, I believe that the Swedish satellite for detecting wildfires is not an EWS, due to it only detecting wildfires once they have started, so I leaned more towards the Silvanet Wildfire System for inspiration for my embedded system, detecting fires that are in the smoldering stage.
